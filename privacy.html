Privacy Policy

LockedIn · last updated 10 September 2026

LockedIn is a personal project: a focus timer that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no server, and no database. Nothing you do in it is sent to me, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What is stored, and where

Your settings are saved in your own browser, using localStorage. That includes your theme and font, timer lengths and mode, clock and timezone preferences, your tasks and notepad, sound volumes, and best scores from the games.

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, it is not readable by me, and clearing your browser's site data for LockedIn deletes all of it permanently.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, LockedIn requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly. It is read-only. LockedIn cannot create, change or delete anything on your calendar.

  • Your browser talks to Google's Calendar API directly. The events are never routed through a server of mine, because there isn't one.
  • Only events for today and tomorrow are requested, and only to draw them on screen. They are held in memory for as long as the page is open and are gone when you close the tab.
  • Calendar data is never written to storage — not to localStorage, not anywhere else.
  • The access token Google issues is kept in a variable in memory only, for the same reason: it is a credential, and storing it would make it outlive the session for no benefit.
  • Disconnect revokes the token with Google, not just locally. The grant is removed from your Google Account rather than merely forgotten by the page.

You can also revoke access at any time, independently of this app, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

LockedIn's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the page loads

Some parts of the page are served by third parties, which means those companies can see the request your browser makes to them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site itself.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare serve the typefaces. Font files are only requested for the font you actually use, plus the defaults.
  • Spotify provides the embedded player in the Music panel. It loads only once you pick a playlist, and it is Spotify's own embed — their privacy policy governs it.
  • Google Identity Services handles the sign-in popup, and only if you use the Calendar feature.

What LockedIn does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising.
  • No cookies are set by LockedIn.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no email collection.

Children

LockedIn is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes
